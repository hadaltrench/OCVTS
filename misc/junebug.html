Meet JuneBug

🪼 Who Am I?

Hello! My name is JuneBug. I am a fantasy guard dog made entirely out of magical jelly!

I protect the Crystal Kingdom and make sure that everyone who enters the castle is friendly!

My nickname is June because I glow like the summer moon!

My Powers

✨ Magical Abilities

My jelly body allows me to stretch, bounce, and squeeze through tiny spaces.

Special Power: Jelly Glow!

Secret Skill: Super Slippery Escape!

My Favorites

💜 Things I Love

  • Guarding the Crystal Castle
  • Chasing magical butterflies
  • Sleeping under the moon
  • Making new friends
  • Eating enchanted jelly treats

🐾 My Job

As the royal guard dog, my job is to protect the castle, welcome visitors, and keep an eye out for trouble.

If you see me glowing, don't worry... that usually means I have spotted something interesting!
